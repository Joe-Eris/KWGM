Biography

Osita Onyeka popularly known as pastor Favour, was born in the year 1992 in Unicha Uthci Ndokwa East L.G.A. of Delta State. He gave his life to christ in 2004 and he was baptised in the Holy Spirirt with the evidence of speaking in tongues. He become the scripture union president during his secondary school day's and youth financial secetary in Peculiar Believer Assembly. He got admission into Edo State Institution Of Science and Technology(Edo State Polytechnic) where he servd as the fellowship president of New LIfe Christain Fellowship.

After graduation he serve as the Resident Pastor of LIberation Power Ministry [AJAFORCE] kwale branch. And by the will and purpose of the Holy Spirit he wa given the mandate to train men through the instrumentality of prayer and the word to experience the true identity in Christ Jesus and that movement is what gave birth to KINGDOM WITNESS GLOBAL MINISTRY.

Awards and Recognition

Over the years of persistent operational dedication, the ministry has been recognized across global ecclesiastical platforms and community leadership boards for outstanding civil support frameworks, educational funding infrastructure, and structural missionary development models.

While the greatest reward remains the testimonies of radical restoration, local councils and internal fellowship associations have honored our foundational pillars with certifications for humanitarian relief excellence, youth optimization leadership setups, and extensive evangelical impact footprint expansion metrics.

Mandate

The core mandate driving Kingdom Witness Global Ministries is captured in a straightforward directive: **To raise a sanctified generation that serves as the physical witness of God's power and sovereignty across modern history.** We are explicitly sent to restore the broken altar of spiritual devotion and raise high-capacity leaders across generations.

This mandate is executed via three distinct pathways: **Proclamation** of the finality of the cross, **Demonstration** of spiritual dominion and deliverance from systemic captivity, and **Deployment** of empowered saints into their prophetic destinies. We stand uncompromised, expanding borders until the kingdoms of this world transform into the kingdoms of our God.
